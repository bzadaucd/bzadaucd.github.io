I am an Assistant Professor at Government College Daggar, Buner, under the Higher Education Department, Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. I hold a Ph.D. in Biochemistry from Gyeongsang National University, South Korea, and completed postdoctoral research at the University of California, Davis, USA. My research focuses on metabolic engineering, synthetic biology, industrial biotechnology, microbial physiology, and bioprocess engineering, with particular emphasis on developing sustainable microbial cell factories for the production of bio-based chemicals, biofuels, and other high-value bioproducts from renewable biomass. Through this website, I share my research, publications, conference presentations, teaching activities, and professional achievements, and welcome opportunities for academic collaboration and interdisciplinary research.

## Research Interests

- Metabolic Engineering
- Synthetic Biology
- Industrial Biotechnology
- Microbial Physiology
- Bioprocess Engineering
- Microbial Cell Factories
- Biomass Valorization
- Bio-based Chemicals and Bioproducts
- Fermentation Technology
