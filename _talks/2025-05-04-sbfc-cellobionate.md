Presented a poster entitled **"Metabolic Engineering of *Thermothelomyces heterothallica* for Cellobionate Production"** at the **47th Symposium on Biomaterials, Fuels and Chemicals (SBFC 2025_SIMB)**, held in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, USA, from **May 4–7, 2025**.

The poster presented research on engineering *Thermothelomyces heterothallica* for sustainable cellobionate production from lignocellulosic biomass using CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing and fermentation optimization.
